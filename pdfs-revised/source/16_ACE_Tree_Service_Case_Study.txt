@h2 Making Room for the Next Generation of Shade

A local homeowner in Birmingham, AL, noticed a mature oak growing too close to their home and needed tree removal services. However, the tree carried personal meaning, and they wanted something new to take its place. An arborist consultation from ACE Tree Service helped them choose a safe location for the replacement tree.

@h2 A Safe Removal Followed by Meaningful Replanting

After safely removing the tree, the crew completed stump grinding and land clearing to create a clean area for the new tree. The team performed tree planting in a location suited for healthy growth. ACE Tree Service also shared tree care tips to help the homeowner look after their new tree.
