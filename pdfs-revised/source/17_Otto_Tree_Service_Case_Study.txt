@h2 Responding to the Homeowner's Wishes for a Safer Yard

Because the tree had already dropped large branches, the homeowner viewed the situation as an emergency and wanted to schedule service as soon as possible. Luckily for them, Otto Tree Service stays ready to respond to these exact kinds of situations.

Upon arrival, the crew assessed the damaged maple and identified multiple unstable limbs still hanging over the yard and home. A professional arborist explained the immediate risks and outlined a clear plan for hazardous tree removal that focused on safety, control, and property protection.

@h2 Another Successful Emergency Tree Removal Project From Otto Tree Service

The team handled the emergency tree removal project by cutting the damaged maple from the top down to control weight and reduce risk. Ropes and rigging guided each section safely away from the home, fence, and nearby structures. After the residential tree removal, the crew cleared the remaining compromised wood and cleaned everything up, leaving the yard safe and ready to use again.
